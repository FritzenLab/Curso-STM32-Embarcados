Blink dev board LED and send UART information

- Onboard LED on pin PC13, using TIM3 at 1ms (variable
counter is used to increase that delay):
htim3.Init.Prescaler = 63;
htim3.Init.Period = 999;

- UART (actually LPUART) on PA9 LPUART_TX / PA10 LPUART_RX
I am sending data every two senconds (an incremental counter) to
the computer. Using the HAL_UART_Transmit_IT(), which is interrupt
based.
